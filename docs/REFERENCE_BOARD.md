for Milestone 0.6 starts here (docs/REALISM_PLAN.md, step 4 onwards). Gathered 31 Oct 2026.

## Herzl Street

**1909–1918** (Soskin, [Herzl 1911](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PikiWiki_Israel_49074_Herzl_street_1911.jpg),
[1911 b](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PikiWiki_Israel_49073_Herzl_street_1911.jpg),
[1910](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PikiWiki_Israel_49072_Herzl_street_1910.jpg),
[1915](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ARAB_DRIVING_HIS_DONKEYS_DOWN_HERZL_STREET_IN_TEL_AVIV._%D7%97%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%97%D7%95%D7%91_%D7%94%D7%A8%D7%A6%D7%9C_%D7%91%D7%AA%D7%9C_%D7%90%D7%91%D7%99%D7%91..jpg);
all public domain)

- **Street:** a light, smoothed roadway with **raised sidewalks and kerbs on both sides already
  in 1911**. Our data has Herzl paved in 1925; see "Layout and data" below.
- **Trees:** saplings along the kerb in **ladder-shaped wooden guards** (two or three ladders
  leaning together).
- **Plot fronts:** low plastered garden walls with square pillars and caps, topped with iron
  railings or wooden pickets; gates between pillars.
- **Lamps:** thin iron posts with a lantern, at the kerb and in the middle of crossings.
- **Houses:** two storeys, red Marseille-tile hip roofs, wooden balconies with iron rails,
  shuttered windows; on the corners, eclectic houses with arched windows and dormered roofs.
- **The Gymnasium** closes the view: two crenellated towers, arched windows in pairs on the
  wings ([1914](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Camel_Taxi_Station.jpg),
  [c. 1920–33](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Herzliya_Hebrew_Gymnasium,_Tel_Aviv.jpg)).
  By 1920 it has cypresses and palms in its front garden behind a fence of posts.

**1930s–1950s:** multi-storey shops with awnings, a busy paved road, traffic police
([1955](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:History_of_Herzl_Street,_Tel_Aviv-Yafo)).

## Rothschild Boulevard

**1910–1919** ([Rothschild 1911](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PikiWiki_Israel_49254_Rothschild_ST.jpg),
[first kiosk](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:First-kiosk-after-1911-before-1919-paintervic.jpg),
[c. 1910](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PikiWiki_Israel_45006_Architecture_of_Israel.jpg)):
a wide sand boulevard with newly planted strips; the first kiosk, a small hexagonal wooden booth
with a pyramid roof; the water tower at the end.

**1920s–1930** ([144596](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:144596_tel_aviv_-_1920s-1930s_PikiWiki_Israel.jpg),
postcards [45653](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PikiWiki_Israel_45653_Rotshild_Boulevard.jpg),
[45655](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PikiWiki_Israel_45655_Rotshild_Boulevard.jpg),
[45649](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PikiWiki_Israel_45649_Rotshild_Boulevard.jpg),
[45651](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PikiWiki_Israel_45651_Rotshild_Boulevard.jpg)):

- **Cross-section:** two sand paths with a long planted bed between them (low shrubs, agaves,
  a round bed at crossings), not one central path.
- **Trees:** rows of **Italian cypress** along both outer edges, 8–12 m, dense and fairly broad;
  younger broadleaf trees **tied to single stakes**.
- **Furniture:** benches along the paths facing them; lamp posts in the bed.
- **By 1930** the broadleaf trees have broad crowns and the boulevard is shaded; a kiosk with an
  umbrella appears ([45651](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PikiWiki_Israel_45651_Rotshild_Boulevard.jpg)).

**1940s** ([1940–46](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tel_Aviv._Rothchild_Ave_1940-46.jpg)):
bare deciduous trees in winter in the central strip, a kiosk with striped awnings.

**Today** (CC BY / BY-SA, reference only:
[2851](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PikiWiki_Israel_2851_Rothschild_Boulevard_Tel-Aviv_%D7%A9%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%98%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%93_%D7%AA%D7%9C-%D7%90%D7%91%D7%99%D7%91.jpg),
[Purim 2012](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Purim_Monring,_Rothschild_Boulevard_P1130255.JPG),
[2014](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tel_Aviv_Yafo_43762_(14451577152).jpg),
[2016](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rothschild_Boulevard,_Tel_Aviv.jpg)):

- **Trees:** **royal poinciana**: low, twisting, often split trunks with smooth light-grey bark,
  a wide umbrella of fine feathery leaves, bare in late winter. **Ficus**: heavy grey
  multi-stem trunks, dense dark evergreen crowns. Planting history: ficus in the 1930s, poinciana
  from the 1920s or later ([UW horticulture library](https://depts.washington.edu/hortlib/calendar/news/scholarJun2020.html)).
- **Ground:** pale stone paving, sand and gravel tree beds, a pétanque strip, a red bike lane,
  red-and-white painted kerbs, concrete bollards, low green hoop fences round lawns.
- **Benches** ([2007](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PikiWiki_Israel_2850_Rothschild_Boulevard_Tel-Aviv_%D7%A9%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%98%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%93_%D7%AA%D7%9C-%D7%90%D7%91%D7%99%D7%91.jpg),
  [2012](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Purim_Monring,_Rothschild_Boulevard_P1130301.JPG),
  [2011](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rothchild_Avenue_(8099937904).jpg)):
  the city bench, wooden slats (dark brown, some painted red) on black cast-iron ends, a
  three-slat back.
- **Kiosks** ([2007](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rothschild_kiosk.JPG),
  [2009](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PikiWiki_Israel_999_Cafe_on_Sderot_Rothschild_%D7%A7%D7%A4%D7%94_%D7%91%D7%A9%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%98%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%93.JPG)):
  white octagonal pavilions with columns and a pyramid roof, opened up as espresso bars with
  bistro chairs round them.

## Textures (photographed, CC0, to ship)

- Leaves: [ambientCG Leaf Sets](https://ambientcg.com/list?q=leaf). LeafSet022 (elliptic,
  entire, dark: ficus), LeafSet019 (scale sprays: cypress), LeafSet002 (small leaflets: poinciana
  pinnae). No fan palm: palm fronds as geometry with a photo texture.
- Bark: ambientCG Bark001–015, to pick a smooth grey one (poinciana, ficus) and a fibrous one
  (cypress).

## Layout and data found wrong on the way

- Herzl had raised sidewalks and kerbs by 1911; `streets.ts` paves it in 1925.
- Rothschild's 1920s boulevard had two paths round a planted bed, and cypress rows; the game has
  one paved middle path and ficus or poinciana from 1911.
- The Ahuzat Bayit plot plan ([Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ahuzat_bait.jpg),
  CC BY-SA 4.0) can check the parcels and street widths of the 1909 core.
